# Code of conduct

GAIA adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are a research collaboration spanning several institutions, career stages and disciplines,
and a good deal of the work happens in public — issues, pull requests, Discussions, recorded
seminars. That makes the ordinary norms of a research group insufficient on their own, because
a graduate student's first public pull request is visible in a way their first group-meeting
question is not. Assume the person on the other end knows something you do not, and say what
you mean without saying it sharply.

**Scope.** This applies in every space the organisation runs — repositories, Discussions,
project Slack, project meetings and recorded seminars — and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

**Reporting.** Report to the Lead PI, or, if the Lead PI is the subject of the report or you
would simply rather not, to `<second contact — name a person at another institution>`.
Reports are handled privately. Naming a second contact who is not the person with budget
authority over most participants is the part of this document that does the actual work;
leaving it as a placeholder makes the rest decorative.

**Consequences** follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder: correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban. Institutional processes at UW, UAF and EarthScope apply
independently of anything decided here, and nothing in this document displaces them.
